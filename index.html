VC-to-English Translator™Full dictionary →
What they said:
"We love the space."
Translation: We are not investing but want to keep tabs on you in case someone else does.
What they said:
"Come back with more traction."
Translation: We have no conviction. Prove us wrong and we'll pretend we were always interested.
What they said:
"It's a valuation issue."
Translation: We do not believe in your company enough to argue about price. Lower it and we still won't invest.
What they said:
"We're close to getting conviction."
Translation: We are 0% close. This is how we buy time without officially passing.
What they said:
"The timing isn't right for us."
Translation: We are at the end of a fund cycle and don't want to admit it. Or: just a no.
What they said:
"We'd love to co-invest alongside a lead."
Translation: We want the upside without the work of due diligence. Find someone else to take the risk first.
